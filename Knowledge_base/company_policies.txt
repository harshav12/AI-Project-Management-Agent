Company Project Management Policies

Project Monitoring Policy

All active projects should be monitored regularly using project progress,
task completion, deadlines, and identified risks.

Overdue Task Policy

A task is considered overdue when its due date has passed and the task has
not been completed. Overdue tasks should be reviewed by the responsible
project team.

Critical Task Policy

Critical-priority tasks require additional attention because delays in
these tasks can have a significant impact on project delivery.

Risk Escalation Policy

Projects with repeated overdue tasks, significant delivery delays, or
multiple critical issues should receive additional management attention.

Project Update Policy

Project updates should communicate meaningful changes in project progress,
delivery risks, blockers, and important milestones.

Project Review Policy

Projects experiencing significant delays or repeated task issues should
be reviewed to identify the cause of the problem and determine appropriate
corrective actions.
